That does not mean every user needs CN2 GIA.

- You are running a private service or development environment.
- The lowest monthly cost matters more than premium China routing.
- You are testing a project before committing to a more expensive network tier.

A CN2 GIA or CTGNet VPS may deserve consideration when:

- A significant portion of your users are in mainland China.
- Packet loss and peak-hour congestion affect the service.
- You operate real-time applications, voice or video services, or online games.
